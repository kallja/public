BLE TPMS Decoder

Paste a captured frame from a BLE tyre pressure sensor (company ID 0x022B). Decoding runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

Formats are reverse-engineered, not officially documented. Pressure is believed accurate to about 1.5%; the advertisement temperature encoding is inferred. A parked sensor usually only advertises a sleep frame without pressure. The car reads pressure over a GATT connection instead.

Decoder ported from the MIT-licensed work of Aliaksandr Anufryieu, building on reverse engineering by cunzulatu.
